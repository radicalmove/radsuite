## ACE RIRs

Source:
http://www.ee.ic.ac.uk/naylor/ACEweb/index.html

License:
Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-ND 4.0)

Reference:
J. Eaton et al., “Estimation of room acoustic parameters: The ACE Challenge,” IEEE/ACM TASLP, 2016.

Redistribution note:
This artifact redistributes a subset (14) of the ACE Challenge room impulse responses unchanged under the original CC BY-ND 4.0 license.

The evaluation scripts locally convolve signals with these RIRs during execution. No modified ACE RIRs are redistributed as part of this artifact.
