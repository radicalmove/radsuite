RADsuite runs locally on your computer and includes RADcite, RADcast, and RADTTS. Choose one installer below.

Which one should I choose?

Only download one. The app will update itself when a newer version is available.

Apple Silicon Mac

For Macs with an M1, M2, M3, or M4 chip.

Download Apple Silicon

Intel Mac

For older Macs with an Intel processor.

Download Intel Mac

Windows

For 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11 computers.

Download Windows

Not sure about your Mac? Open the Apple menu, choose About This Mac, and look for either “Chip” (Apple Silicon) or “Processor” (Intel).

What happens next?

Open the downloaded installer and follow the normal installation steps. RADsuite keeps its work on the local computer.
