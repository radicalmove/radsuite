Which Mac do I have?

On your Mac, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. Then check which label appears:

Choose Apple Silicon Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3, or M4
Choose Intel Mac Processor: Intel

What happens next?

Open the downloaded installer and follow the normal installation steps. RADsuite keeps its work on the local computer.
